##### Engines
*LY7 / LFY 3.6L V6*
This naturally aspirated aluminium block dominates the mid-spec market and rewards conservative servicing. The primary failure vector sits in the coolant bypass valve housing, where composite polymer blends degrade under sustained high-cycle thermal expansion, leading to internal weeping past the heater core return. High-pressure fuel pumps exhibit wobble bearing wear past 140,000 km, manifesting as hard starting and rough idle enrichment events. Timing chain hydraulic tensioners lose residual pressure if oil change windows stretch beyond factory limits, producing audible rattle on cold crank. Preventative maintenance demands 10,000 km service intervals with Dexos II specification synthetic lubricants to preserve variable valve timing phaser clearance and prevent sludge migration into camshaft galleries.

*LLT 6.0L V8*
Built for peak torque delivery rather than fractional fuel economy, the LS-based displacement mill thrives under high-load duty but demands vigilance around ancillary plumbing. Positive crankcase ventilation (PCV) diaphragms split, drawing unfiltered air into intake trumpets and enriching mixture trims excessively. Direct injection carburetton builds carbon deposits on intake valve faces, reducing combustion efficiency and increasing knock sensor retarding. Oil cooler peripheral gaskets fail as aluminum mating surfaces warp from overheating events, flooding the accessory drive belt with lubricant. Maintenance cycles stretch to 15,000 km due to larger sump volumes and extended-life coolant chemistry, though track use or towing mandates halving that window to preserve valve stem seals and piston ring land integrity.

Fluid flush protocols require dedicated bi-directional scan tool activation to purge legacy ATF from cooler circuits, otherwise oxidised residues circulate through newly installed clutches. 

The 8-speed Hydra-Matic (**HYPTM81**) introduced later offers broader ratio spread and improved highway refinement, but trades simplicity for computational dependency.
